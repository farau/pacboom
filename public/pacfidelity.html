PAC Fidelity

Are you a true member of the Pacmoon community? Let's assess your PAC fidelity!

What is the PAC Fidelity Program?

The PAC Fidelity Program is a way to measure your engagement with the Pacmoon community. Here are some details about the program:

  • Points are updated once a day.
  • Points are calculated based on the tweets you've made about Pacmoon and your interactions with the PAC community.
  • The more points you have, the higher the rarity of your Pacmoon PFP will be!
  • Additional benefits will be available depending on the rarity of your Pacmoon PFP! Perhaps $PAC rewards?
  • And most importantly, this tool can be used by Social Validators to gauge the engagement of the PAC community!

How to register?

  1. Connect your Twitter account.
  2. Register your Pacmoon PFP here.
  3. Then, simply be active on Twitter to accumulate points.
