How Staking Bonuses Work

ETH Locking Bonus:
  • Base Bonus:
    • If you lock more than 0.05 ETH, you get 1 bonus point.
    • If you lock more than 0.1 ETH, the bonus is capped at 2 bonus points.
  • Additional Bonus Based on Duration:
    • 14-day lock period: If you lock more than 0.2 ETH, you get 1 additional bonus point.
    • 28-day lock period: If you lock 1 ETH or more, you get 2 additional bonus points.
NFT Ownership Bonus:
  • If you own fewer than 5 NFTs, you get 1 bonus point.
  • If you own 5 NFTs or more, you get 2 bonus points.
Total Potential Bonus:

By combining all the bonuses, you can earn up to 7 bonus points:

  • 2 base points for ETH locked
  • 2 additional points for the maximum lock duration of 28 days
  • 2 points for the NFTs you own

Example: If you lock 1 ETH for 28 days and own 5 NFTs or more, you’ll receive the maximum of 7 bonus points.

Note: the bonus updates at each lock or when you claim PacBoom points.
