Boss Statistics

Statistic Description
Serial The serial number that identifies the boss.
Life The remaining life of the boss.
Prize The prize associated with the boss. The prize will be awarded to the killer of the boss!
Entry Fee The cost in Blast tokens to attack the boss.
Damage The damage dealt by the boss in PacBoom points. This means that each time you attack the boss, your number of PB points will be decreased by this damage.

To attack the boss, you need to have a number of PacBoom points greater than the damage statistic; otherwise, you won't be able to attack it.
